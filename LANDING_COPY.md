## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. The audit runs entirely in your browser. Your tool and spend data is only saved to your device (localStorage) unless you choose to enter your email at the end.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price in the audit engine traces back to an official vendor pricing page, verified during submission week. See PRICING_DATA.md for the full citation list. We update the data when vendors change their plans.

**Q: Will you sell my email or spam me?**
A: No. If you submit your email, you get one email: your audit report. If your savings are over $500/mo, a Credex team member may reach out about discounted AI credits — that's it. No newsletter, no drip campaigns.

**Q: What's Credex and why are they offering this for free?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits sourced from companies that over-forecast. SpendLens is free because it's genuinely useful — and for teams with significant AI spend, Credex can often help them save even more beyond the plan optimizations this tool identifies.

**Q: My AI spend is complicated — we use the API directly. Can this tool help?**
A: Yes. SpendLens supports Anthropic API and OpenAI API direct usage and can compare your API spend to equivalent flat-plan costs. For teams primarily using the API for automation, it may confirm that pay-per-token is the right choice — and that's a useful output too.
